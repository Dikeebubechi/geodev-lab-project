# Project Brief: Urban Growth in Asaba, Delta State (2000–2025)

## 1. Spatial Question

How much has the built-up area of Asaba, Delta State, Nigeria expanded between 2000 and 2025, and in which directions has the expansion occurred?

This project will examine the changes in built-up areas in Asaba over the period 2000 to 2025. It will measure the amount of built-up land for selected years and identify the main directions and areas where urban expansion has taken place.

## 2. Study Area

The study will focus on Asaba, Delta State, Nigeria, which is located on the western bank of the River Niger, opposite Onitsha in Anambra State.

The main study area will cover Oshimili South Local Government Area, including adjoining parts of Oshimili North where urban development extends beyond the main built-up area.

The approximate central coordinates of Asaba are 6.20°N, 6.73°E.

Asaba was selected because it is the capital of Delta State and has experienced considerable urban development over the years. Its location along major transportation routes, including the Asaba–Onitsha corridor, also makes it suitable for studying the pattern and direction of urban expansion.

## 3. Datasets

### 3.1 Landsat 5 TM Surface Reflectance

Years: 2000, 2005 and 2010
Purpose:
Landsat 5 imagery will be used to map the built-up area and other land-cover classes during the earlier period of the study.

Source:

Google Earth Engine Landsat 5: https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/landsat-5

### 3.2 Landsat 8 OLI Surface Reflectance
Years:2015 and 2020

Purpose:Landsat 8 imagery will be used to map built-up areas and other land-cover classes during the middle and later periods of the study.

Source:

Google Earth Engine Landsat 8: https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/landsat-8

### 3.3 Landsat 9 OLI-2 Surface Reflectance

Year:2025

Purpose:
Landsat 9 imagery will be used to map the most recent built-up area and provide the 2025 reference for comparison with the earlier years.

Source:

Google Earth Engine Landsat 9: https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/landsat-9

### 3.4 Nigeria Administrative Boundaries
Purpose:
Administrative boundary data will be used to identify and define the study area, particularly Oshimili South and the adjoining Oshimili North area.

Source:
GADM: https://gadm.org/download_country.html

Humanitarian Data Exchange: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-nga

### 3.5 OpenStreetMap Road Network

Purpose:
Road data will be used to examine the relationship between urban expansion and major transportation routes around Asaba.

Source:
Geofabrik Nigeria: https://download.geofabrik.de/africa/nigeria.html


## 4. Summary

This project will use multi-temporal Landsat satellite imagery to examine the growth of built-up areas in Asaba between 2000 and 2025. The analysis will show how much the built-up area has increased, where the expansion has occurred, and the main directions of urban growth. Road network data will also be used to examine the relationship between urban expansion and major transportation routes.
