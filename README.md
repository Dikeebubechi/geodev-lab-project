Urban Growth in Asaba, Delta State (2000–2025)

About the Project:

This repository contains the project brief for a spatial analysis of urban growth in Asaba, Delta State, Nigeria, between 2000 and 2025.
The project focuses on changes in built-up areas and the spatial direction of urban expansion during the study period.

The study investigates:

How much has the built-up area of Asaba, Delta State, Nigeria expanded between 2000 and 2025, and in which directions has the expansion occurred?

Study Area:

The study focuses on Asaba, Delta State, Nigeria, with the main study area covering Oshimili South Local Government Area and adjoining parts of Oshimili North where urban expansion has occurred.

Data: 

The project uses the following main datasets:
•	Landsat 5 TM Surface Reflectance
•	Landsat 8 OLI Surface Reflectance
•	Landsat 9 OLI-2 Surface Reflectance
•	Nigeria Administrative Boundaries
•	OpenStreetMap Road Network

Detailed information about each dataset, including its purpose and source, is provided in the project-brief.md
